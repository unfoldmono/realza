Why Unfold?

A raw prompt gives you a guess. Unfold already knows your brand, so everything it builds is consistent and on par with your best brand work.

Trained on design, not just words

The AI behind Unfold is built to understand layout, type, colour and how designs get implemented, so the output holds up when you look closely.

Revisions like working with people

Leave a note the way you would for a designer. Unfold comes back with a new version and tells you what it changed and why.

Version control you can read

Every change is a visible version. Compare, branch, roll back, and swap one section or element for another without breaking the rest.

A whole company, working for you

Art direction, design, development and motion run as one team on one canvas, and you can watch every step.
